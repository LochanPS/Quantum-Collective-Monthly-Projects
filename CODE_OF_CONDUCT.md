# Code of Conduct

## Pledge

Participation in Quantum Collective Monthly Projects is harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Standards

**Do:**
- Give constructive, specific feedback
- Help beginners — quantum computing is hard for everyone at first
- Credit others' ideas
- Ask questions openly — no question is too basic

**Don't:**
- Belittle others' code, questions, or experience level
- Post off-topic content in issues or discussions
- Harass anyone in any form

## Enforcement

Report violations by opening an Issue titled `[conduct]` or contacting the maintainer directly via GitHub.

Maintainers may hide comments, close discussions, or remove users who repeatedly violate this code.
